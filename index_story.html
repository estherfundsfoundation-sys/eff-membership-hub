WHY EFF EXISTS

Most students who leave college could have stayed.

They don't leave because they can't do the work. They leave because of a bill nobody explained, a hold nobody lifted, an aid letter nobody translated, a semester nobody asked about. Esther Funds Foundation exists so that never happens to a student alone.

01

PICTURE IT

The semester that almost ended it.

SophomoreFirst-generationA hold on her accountNobody to ask

Picture it. Week three. A sophomore opens her student portal and sees a red banner: financial aid hold. Her classes could be dropped by Friday. She has no idea what the hold is, who put it there, or who takes it off. Her mom went to college in another country. Her roommate shrugs. She starts drafting the text: "I think I'm going to take a semester off."

Then she remembers the EFF member she met at a tabling event. She opens MyEFF. Inside: REACH, the hold triage tool, and a chapter president two dorms away who says, "That's the financial aid office. Here's exactly what to ask. Want me to walk over with you?"

"I didn't need money that day. I needed someone who knew the building."
02

WHAT MEMBERSHIP ACTUALLY IS

Not a card. A crew.

EFF membership is free, national, and real. It comes with people who know your name, a framework called REACH — Reach Out, Engage Your Community, Access Resources, Care for Your Mental Health, Hold On — and tools that turn a crisis into a checklist. It comes with a chapter if your campus has one, and a reason to start one if it doesn't. And it comes with expectations: show up, serve with integrity, protect the culture. No hazing. Ever.

"You do not have to be finished to belong here. You do not have to be perfect to serve."
