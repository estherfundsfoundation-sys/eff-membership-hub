Membership path

Four steps from first login to active member. Your home shows where you are and what's next.

STEP 1Create your profile

Name, school, and pathway. Any college or university — type it if it isn't listed.

STEP 2Roster verification

Register on the free national roster (Join It). EFF National verifies your record.

STEP 3Complete the Esther Experience

Eight modules. Unlocks induction clearance and member-only scholarship eligibility.

STEP 4Serve and lead

Log service, join chapter life, and step into officer, advisor, or regional roles.
